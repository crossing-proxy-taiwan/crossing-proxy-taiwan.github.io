FAQ

Read what we do not do before you ask.

Can I read the site in English and still look at services for Vietnam?

Vietnam-specific services are on the Tiếng Việt page, together with services for every nationality. Choose Tiếng Việt in the language menu and both the language and the service filter change. English shows the shared services.

Do you file visas, residence permits, or work permits?

We do not act as an immigration, visa, or residence-permit agent, and we do not promise any government result. Document help means explaining, organizing, and translating. Work that needs a legal qualification is not labeled as a partner referral until a real lawful partner is confirmed.

How much does it cost?

Prices are not published. There is no starting price on this site. Ask on LINE. We explain how the fee is calculated before we begin.

What cannot be shipped to Vietnam?

Meat and meat products are not accepted; batteries and power banks are restricted; liquids and medicine are usually restricted too. Rules change, so confirm again before you ship.

Can I rent a scooter or furniture?

Not yet. Short- and medium-term scooter rental, and household or furniture rental, are planned. An inquiry only records interest. It is not a booking.

Do you handle money transfers?

No. We do not collect or send money. If social posts compared legal transfer options, that was general information, not a paid service on this site.

Does this website store the form?

No. The page is static. After you copy the text, it stays on your device until you paste it into LINE. Do not enter an ID, passport, ARC number, or other sensitive data.

Can foreign residents ask about a scooter license test or exchanging a foreign license for a Taiwan license?

Yes, as an inquiry. That can cover booking a test, registering a retake, the exchange steps, organizing papers, written-test notes and traffic rules, and motor vehicle office procedures. Translation and accompaniment are separate questions and are confirmed case by case. Eligibility and documents follow Taiwan's motor vehicle authority. We do not guarantee a pass or a successful exchange.
