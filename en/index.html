Questions

Things people often ask

Does the first consultation cost anything?

The first consultation is free. If you need the actual service, we explain the work and the fee, and we start only after you agree.

Which languages can I use?

A person can reply in 繁體中文, English, Tiếng Việt, Bahasa Indonesia, ไทย, Filipino, and हिन्दी. You can switch the site language anytime. A language on the site does not mean every professional task is done in that language.

Does this website collect my information?

There is no inquiry form. The site does not collect, store, or send personal details typed into the site. Free consultation opens LINE. Later messages on LINE are handled by staff of 懿翔實業有限公司. Your language and country stay on your device.

Can you file a visa or give legal advice?

We do not handle visas or licensed immigration work, we do not give legal advice, and we do not handle money transfers. When a qualification is required, we help you reach a suitable, lawful service. Names and licenses of those parties are not published here.

What are the hours?

Hours are 08:00–23:00, and a first reply is usually within an hour.

Why are there no numbers on the price table?

Where a fixed price is not published, the page says the first consultation is free and a formal quote comes after we confirm what you need. Unconfirmed numbers are not put on the site.
