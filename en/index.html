FAQ

Read what we do not do before you ask.

If I read the site in English, can I still find Vietnam services?

Yes. Change the language at the top, then set the country to Vietnam. Words and the service list are separate.

Do you file visas, residence permits, or work permits?

We do not act as an immigration, visa, or residence-permit agent, and we do not promise any government result. Document help means explaining, organizing, and translating. Work that needs a legal qualification is not labeled as a partner referral until a real licensed partner is confirmed.

How much does it cost?

Prices are not published. There is no starting price on this site. Ask on LINE. We explain how the fee is calculated before we begin.

What cannot be shipped to Vietnam?

From the owner's own public guidance: meat and meat products are not accepted; batteries and power banks are restricted; liquids and medicine are usually restricted too. Rules change, so confirm again before you ship. The full prohibited-item text from the old website could not be recovered from the suspended account, so this is not that original list.

Can I rent a scooter or furniture?

Not yet. Short- and medium-term scooter rental, and household or furniture rental, are planned. An inquiry only records interest. It is not a booking.

Do you handle money transfers?

No. We do not collect or send money. If social posts compared legal transfer options, that was general information, not a paid service on this site.

Does this website store the form?

No. The page is static. After you copy the text, it stays on your device until you paste it into LINE. Do not enter an ID, passport, ARC number, or other sensitive data.
